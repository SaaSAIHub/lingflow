# LingFlow Workspace, Credits and Privacy

[Back to LingFlow overview](../README.md)

LingFlow organizes PDF and image translation in projects and a central file library. Translation requires an account and server-side processing; it is not a browser-only local tool.

## Projects and the file library

Sign in with Google or register using an email address at the [workspace](https://workspace.lingflow.ai/). Create a project, add local files or select library files, configure languages, review the credit estimate and start translation. Open the project to follow progress, preview completed results and download available files.

The library lets you find, filter, rename, preview and download source files and translations. Here, linguistic asset management refers to keeping these assets organized and available for later review and reuse.

## Understand three kinds of credit validity

| Credit type | Validity and purchase terms |
| --- | --- |
| New-user trial | 1,000 credits, valid for one calendar month from registration. Month-end dates are adjusted; check the account's exact expiry. |
| 30-day pass | Credits expire 30 days after payment confirmation, including unused credits. One-time payment, no auto-renewal. |
| Credit pack | Credits never expire. One-time purchase, no auto-renewal. |

These are different validity rules: a calendar month is not always 30 days. The current pricing FAQ says credits with the earliest expiry are used first and permanent credits last. It also says another pass or an upgrade is unavailable while a pass remains valid; credit packs can be added as needed.

Standard rates are 10 credits per PDF page and 30 credits per image translation task. PDF billing uses the page count verified during processing. Review the estimate before starting. The current pricing FAQ states that failed translations return credits; contact support if the balance does not match the task outcome.

Use the [pricing page](https://lingflow.ai/pricing) for current prices and terms. This guide does not reproduce changing promotional price tables or promise unlimited free use.

## File handling and privacy

The [LingFlow privacy policy](https://lingflow.ai/privacy-policy) states that uploaded source files and translated documents are stored in the account for access, management and download, and users can delete files from their account. It does not establish the 24-hour retention window used by some other tools.

The policy says translated documents are not used to train LingFlow's core AI models. It also describes account information, usage data, cookies and limited disclosures to service providers or where legally required. Read the full policy rather than interpreting a short marketing statement as a promise of no third-party processing.

Before uploading, confirm you may process the file and remove unnecessary private information. Download results you need for your own records. Manage files in the workspace when they are no longer needed; this guide does not make claims about immediate deletion from every backup or log.
